# Character Bible

| ID | Name | Age | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALX | Alex Morgan | 27 | Protagonist, player character |
| MAY | Maya Reed | 28 | Ally, Alex's best friend, paramedic (living world) |
| ARV | The Archivist | — | Guide, Index curator |
| ARC | The Architect (Dr. Elias Morgan) | 58 | Antagonist, Alex's father |
| FCH | The Forgotten Child (Wren Morgan) | 9 | Secret at the center of the world |

## Alex Morgan
- **Want:** know who they are. **Need:** forgive themself.
- **Voice:** dry, guarded, short sentences; softens when talking about Maya or water.
- **Look:** dark coat, scuffed boots, a thin scar over left eyebrow, a missing wristwatch tan line.
- **Arc:** denial → grief → acceptance. Player-defined tone via dialogue choices.

## Maya Reed
- **Want:** wake Alex up. **Need:** let Alex choose.
- **Voice:** warm, funny under stress, medical vocabulary slips out ("vitals", "stable").
- **Look:** red jacket, hospital band on left wrist, braid.
- **In Echo City:** appears as a memory-echo, then as real signal from Ch4. Can be wrong about things the Index feeds her.

## The Archivist
- **Want:** see the Index ended without breaking its rules. **Need:** to be believed.
- **Voice:** formal, courteous, speaks in catalogue phrasing ("Item: your name. Condition: misplaced.").
- **Look:** tall figure of paper and glass, face an old index-card drawer. Light flickers when it breaks a rule.

## The Architect
- **Want:** keep Wren alive forever. **Need:** to say goodbye.
- **Voice:** gentle, reasonable, never shouts; most frightening when kind.
- **Look:** gray cardigan, lab coat, a tuning fork he always holds.
- **Belief:** erasing pain is mercy.

## The Forgotten Child (Wren)
- **Voice:** quiet, uses questions. Hums a lullaby repeatedly (the audio motif of the game).
- **Look:** yellow raincoat, bare feet, wet footprints that dry.

## The Forgotten (creatures)
Faceless memory-echoes. Never attack; they cluster, block paths, and whisper erased lines. Mercy or avoidance affects `forgotten_mercy`.
