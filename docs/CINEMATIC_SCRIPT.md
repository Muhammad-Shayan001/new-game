# Cinematic Script

Format: `[CINE_ID] shot · camera · audio · dialogue refs`

## CINE_01 — "Static" (Ch1 intro, 45 s)
1. Black. Radio static, a faint lullaby hum.
2. Slow push into Echo Station bench; Alex's eyes open.
3. Terminal flickers: **YOU WERE NEVER SUPPOSED TO REMEMBER.**
4. Alex stands; camera pulls back to a wide shot of the empty platform. Title card: AFTERLIFE.EXE.
Audio: ambient wind, 4:17 clock ticking.

## CINE_02 — "The Archive" (Ch1 end, 60 s)
Shots: wide of Archive Tower, close on the blank index card, Archivist offers the card. Dialogue CH1_ARV_006.

## CINE_03 — "A Chair Without a Name" (Ch2 end, 40 s)
Low-angle on the empty chair; Maya places her hand on it. Dialogue CH2_MAY_004.

## CINE_04 — "Shift" (Ch3, 30 s)
Reality Shift first-use: environment dissolves to wireframe memory, then reforms with nursery wallpaper.

## CINE_05 — "Bedside" (Ch4, 75 s)
Cut between Maya's hand on Alex's hand and Alex watching from the Echo. Heartbeat monitor sound synced to the lullaby.

## CINE_06 — "The Atrium" (Ch5, 60 s)
Wide crane through a cathedral of suspended memories; the Architect appears turning his tuning fork.

## CINE_07–10 — Endings (Ch6)
- **Save:** warm light, Alex wakes, Wren's laugh in the hallway.
- **Release:** city fading to white, Wren waves from the shore, Alex opens eyes to Maya.
- **Escape:** Alex in a corridor forgetting a name; chilly cut to hospital ceiling.
- **Secret:** Wren walks out of the water into daylight beside Alex; the empty chair is filled; fade to credits.

## Technical notes
Cinematics run real-time in-engine (Godot AnimationPlayer + camera tracks); skippable, subtitle-enabled; budgeted at ≤ 3 m of total camera movement per shot for mobile performance.
