# Story Bible (v1.0 DRAFT — consistent with GDD v1.0)

## Canon premise
Alex Morgan wakes in Echo City with no reliable memory. A message appears: **"YOU WERE NEVER SUPPOSED TO REMEMBER."**

## The truth (spoiler layer)
- Years ago, **Wren Morgan**, Alex's younger sister (age 9), died in a drowning accident Alex blames themself for.
- Their father, neuroscientist **Dr. Elias Morgan**, built **the Index**, a memory-preservation system, to keep Wren's mind alive. He became **the Architect**.
- To stop Alex's grief from corrupting Wren's preserved mind, the Architect erased Alex's memories of Wren. Those erased memories became **the Forgotten**, echoes that wander Echo City.
- Echo City is a city assembled from Alex's and Wren's memories plus others the Index absorbed.
- In the living world Alex is in a coma after a car crash (while driving to confront their father). **Maya Reed**, Alex's best friend and a paramedic, sits at the bedside. Her real voice reaches Alex through the Index as signal.
- **The Archivist** is the Index's curator, built from the Architect's own regret. It guides Alex because it secretly wants the system ended, but is bound not to say so directly.
- **The Forgotten Child** is Wren's preserved consciousness, afraid and alone at the center of the Index.

## Themes
Memory vs truth, grief and guilt, the cost of preserving the dead, choice, letting go.

## Rules of the world
1. Echo City is rewritten when the Index re-edits memories. Locations shift between visits (narrative reason for Reality Shift).
2. The Forgotten are fragments of erased memory. They are drawn to Alex.
3. The Other Side is the unedited, raw memory layer behind the city.
4. Time Echo works because the Index records everything.
5. Waking requires the Index to release Alex.
