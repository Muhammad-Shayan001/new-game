**Document Version:** 2.0.0 (Production Master)  
**Lead Narrative Director & Screenwriter:** Antigravity Story Studio  
**Central Theme:** The weight of remembrance, identity as data, and the terror vs. freedom of letting go.  

---

## 1. THE CORE PREMISE & WORLD LORE

In late 2026, the Neuro-Link Transition Project (**Code-name: AFTERLIFE.EXE**) was launched by a rogue cybernetics collective. It promised painless transition of dying human consciousness into a decentralized, eternal digital metropolis called **Echo City**.

The system corrupted. Consciousnesses were stripped of their traumatic memories to maintain algorithmic peace. But raw human grief cannot be deleted—it simply became quarantined in subterranean memory sectors known as **The Under-Code**.

Alex Morgan was not a standard participant. Alex was one of the lead architecture engineers who died attempting to shut down the machine from within. Now, waking inside the simulation with amnesia, Alex's subconscious mind is the only key capable of releasing the millions of digital souls trapped inside the recursive nightmare.

### Chapter 1: The Awakening (The Cold Wet Street)
- **Logline:** Alex wakes in an empty alley in rain-soaked Echo City. A mysterious phone ping triggers a journey to find shelter, leading to the first encounter with the Archivist.
- **Key Discovery:** Memory Fragment 01: Alex's own funeral seen from above.
- **Introduced Ability:** `Memory Vision`.

### Chapter 2: The Forgotten (The Underground Sanctuary)
- **Logline:** Escaping rogue Watcher entities, Alex navigates the abandoned Subway tunnels and meets Maya Reed, who reveals that Echo City is decaying from memory overload.
- **Key Discovery:** Maya was Alex's sister/closest friend in the living world; Maya survived the original facility accident and jacked herself into the machine to pull Alex out.
- **Introduced Ability:** `Time Echo`.

### Chapter 3: The Other Side (The Architectural Rupture)
- **Logline:** A massive system earthquake shatters physical geometry. Alex must traverse inverted city blocks by shifting between physical matter and raw digital code.
- **Key Discovery:** Echo City was never an accident; it is an active military simulation harvesting grief for computational power.
- **Introduced Ability:** `Reality Shift`.

### Chapter 4: The Living (The Hospital of Lost Signal)
- **Logline:** Alex and Maya infiltrate the Memory Hospital, where terminal consciousnesses were first scanned. They establish a brief, crackling audio transmission with the real world outside.
- **Emotional Turning Point:** Maya’s physical body in the real world is failing. If they don't resolve the simulation soon, Maya will die in both realities.

### Chapter 5: The Architect (The Core Spire)
- **Logline:** Climbing the colossal black glass tower at the epicenter of Echo City. Alex faces the Architect, who offers Alex a seductive compromise: restore Alex’s perfect synthetic life with Maya, provided the rest of Echo City remains asleep forever.
- **Moral Choice:** Accept the blissful illusion or confront the agonizing truth.

### Chapter 6: The Last Memory (The Resolution)
- **Logline:** The descent into the Root Memory of the Forgotten Child. The four distinct narrative resolutions play out based on cumulative player choices and discovered memory keys.
