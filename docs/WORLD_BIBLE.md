Consistent with STORY_BIBLE.md. Locations use IDs from LOCATION_REGISTER.md.

## What the world is
Echo City is a city generated by **the Index** from the memories of Alex and Wren Morgan, plus fragments of other people the Index absorbed. It looks like an ordinary city at dusk, but its clocks all read **4:17** (the minute of Alex's crash), and its streets quietly rearrange between visits.

## Rules of the world
1. **Memory builds space.** Places exist because someone remembered them; erased memories leave gaps (the missing chair, the blank card).
2. **The Index edits.** Locations can be rewritten when memories are edited; story-driven changes between visits are intentional.
3. **The Forgotten** are erased memories given form. Non-hostile; they cluster around Alex.
4. **The Other Side (Dreamland)** is the raw, unedited memory layer behind the city.
5. **Time is recorded, not linear.** The Index records everything, which is why Time Echo works.
6. **Water is the wound.** Water imagery (waves, lakes, flooding) escalates as the truth approaches.
7. **Waking requires release.** Only the Index can release Alex; the ending depends on how it is released.

## History of Echo City (in-world)
- **Before:** Echo City is not a real place; the **Sunken District** is the oldest layer, shaped from the lab town where Dr. Elias Morgan built the Index (Old City Hall = his lab).
- **Early:** only Wren's memories formed the Index. The water-filled streets reflect the lake.
- **Later:** Alex's coma pulled Alex in; the Index built the dusk city around them using the apartment, the metro Alex rode, and the park Wren loved.
- **Erased districts:** the **Forgotten Quarter** was the part of town where Alex and Wren lived; its seats are missing because those memories were removed.
